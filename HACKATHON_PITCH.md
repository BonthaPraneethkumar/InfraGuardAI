# InfraGuard AI — 90-second demo story

**Problem**
Citizens see potholes, broken streetlights, leaks, garbage, exposed wires and traffic
problems every day. Reporting is fragmented, and complaints can be routed incorrectly.

**Solution**
InfraGuard AI creates one simple reporting path. A citizen captures evidence, speaks
naturally and shares location. A privacy gate runs before the case continues. Sarvam
speech AI converts the voice into text, and Sarvam-105B recommends the issue type,
severity and correct department.

**Human-in-the-loop**
The AI assists; it does not replace the government officer. The officer reviews the
case, updates its status and provides resolution evidence.

**Citizen trust**
The citizen can track the same case from registration through resolution.

**Management value**
A single analytics view shows workload, severity and department distribution.

**Closing line**
InfraGuard AI turns scattered complaints into privacy-aware, intelligent and accountable action.
